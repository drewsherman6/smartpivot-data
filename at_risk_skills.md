# At-Risk Skills (High Automation Risk 2026–2030)

This document identifies skills and tasks with high probability of significant automation due to AI and robotics.

## High-Risk Skills & Tasks

### 1. Routine Data Entry & Administrative Processing
- **O*NET Skills:** Data Entry, Record Keeping
- **Risk Level:** very_high
- **Rationale:** AI agents and RPA systems can already perform these tasks faster and more accurately.
- **Related Occupations:**
  - 43-9061.00 Office Clerks, General
  - 43-6014.00 Secretaries and Administrative Assistants

### 2. Basic Content Generation (Reports, Summaries)
- **O*NET Skills:** Writing (routine), Information Ordering
- **Risk Level:** high
- **Rationale:** Large language models now generate first drafts of reports, marketing copy, and documentation at scale.
- **Related Occupations:**
  - 43-4171.00 Receptionists and Information Clerks

### 3. Simple Customer Service & Query Handling
- **O*NET Skills:** Customer Service, Query Resolution
- **Risk Level:** high

- **Related Occupations:**
  - 43-4051.00 Customer Service Representatives

### 4. Basic Code Generation & Maintenance
- **O*NET Skills:** Programming (routine)
- **Risk Level:** high

  - 15-1256.00 Computer Programmers

  - 51-9061.00 Inspectors, Testers, Sorters, Samplers, and Weighers
